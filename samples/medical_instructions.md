# Taking your medication safely

Your clinician has prescribed a medication to help manage hypertension and reduce the incidence of cardiovascular events. It is important that you understand the dosage, the administration schedule, and the adverse effects that you should report.

## Before you start

Tell your clinician about any comorbidity, including diabetes, renal impairment, or a history of syncope. If you are pregnant or breastfeeding, ask about pharmacokinetic considerations and the safety profile for the fetus.

## While you take it

Take the tablet orally at the same time each day. Do not discontinue the medication abruptly, as discontinuation may precipitate a hypertensive crisis or angina. Titration is required in the first four weeks.

## Call your clinician right away if you have

- Severe dizziness, syncope, or dyspnea
- Swelling, or edema in the face or throat
- A rash, or any sign of anaphylaxis
- Irregular heartbeat, or arrhythmia

If you miss a dose, do not double the next dose. Contact your pharmacist for guidance.
