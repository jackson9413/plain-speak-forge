# How refunds work

You can request a refund within 30 days of the charge. We process refunds to the original payment method. Most refunds arrive within 5 business days, though your bank may take another 3 to 5 days to post them.

## When you can get a refund

- The order has not shipped.
- The item is unused and in its original packaging.
- You bought it from our store, not from a marketplace partner.

## If the item arrived damaged

Send us a photo within 7 days. We reply within one business day. If the damage happened in transit, we will send a replacement. If we cannot replace the item, we will refund you in full.

## If you change your mind

We cannot accept returns on perishable items, custom items, or downloaded software. We do not restock opened hygiene products.

For orders over $75, we cover the return shipping. For smaller orders, return shipping is yours to pay.
