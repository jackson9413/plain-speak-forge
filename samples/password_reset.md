# Reset your password

Your password reset link works once. It expires 30 minutes after we send it.

## Steps

1. Open the email we sent you.
2. Click "Reset password".
3. Type a new password. Use at least 12 characters.
4. Click Save.

You will be signed out on every device when the password changes.

## If the link does not work

Check your spam folder. If the email is not there, request a new link from the sign-in page. A new link invalidates the old one.

If you still cannot sign in, ask your workspace admin to reset it for you. Admins cannot see your old password.

## Keep your account safe

Turn on two-factor sign-in. We will never ask for your password by email or phone.
